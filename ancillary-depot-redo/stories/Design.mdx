# Ancillary Depot

Circular storage drum + radially arranged body pods. Entire asset. Strong silhouette, few parts, readable from far away.

## 1. Warning On The Frontier
*"Hold your advance. This isn't a fort, and it isn't scrap. Whatever this is, nothing else is built around it."*

## 2. The Vault Revealed
Large thick-walled circular drum. Around its full circumference: large vertical body-shaped storage pods arranged like cartridges around a revolver cylinder. Each pod front has a simple blocky humanoid silhouette — dormant Ancillary bodies sealed inside.

## 3. The Marks Of The Hive
- **Blackened iron** — drum and pod bodies
- **Aged brass** — rim bands, pod collars, edges
- **Cyan aether** — very small recessed lights at seam junctions only

Chunky low-poly hard-surface. Form and silhouette first.

## 4. Waiting To Be Used
Sealed. No open interior. Spares held in reserve. 3D and 2D top-down share identical footprint and silhouette.

## Specs
- **Composition:** Drum + radial humanoid body pods (entire asset)
- **Style:** Chunky low-poly, futuristic steampunk industrial
- **3D:** ~250–500 tris, watertight, single mesh, bottom-centered pivot
- **2D:** Top-down, flat, high-contrast, transparent, readable at 32×32 / 64×64 / 128×128
