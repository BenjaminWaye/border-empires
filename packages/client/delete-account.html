Border Empires · Account deletion

Delete your account

Deleting your account removes your login immediately. Your profile, associated game data and social data will be deleted or anonymised within 30 days. Shared match outcomes may remain anonymously. Backup copies expire within 30 days after completion. Deletion is permanent.

You can also open Settings → Account → Delete account inside the Android, iOS or browser game. For an unsaved guest account, use that option on the device where you played.
