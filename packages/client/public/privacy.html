Border Empires

Privacy Policy

Your empire, your data.

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Border Empires is operated by Benjamin Waye. For privacy questions, email benjamin@borderempires.com.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers the Border Empires Android and iOS apps and browser game. It explains how we process information when you play as a guest, save an empire, sign in, or contact support.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information such as your email address, authentication provider, display name, and Firebase account identifier.
  • Gameplay information such as territory ownership, progression, resources, alliances, messages, combat outcomes, and other in-game state tied to your account.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, coarse location inferred from network data, and diagnostic logs necessary to operate, secure, and improve the game.
  • Support information you choose to send if you contact us with bug reports or account issues.

3. How we use your information

We process information needed to provide the game under our agreement with you, to protect and improve the service based on our legitimate interests, to comply with legal obligations, and with consent where required.

  • To authenticate you and keep your empire persistent across sessions and devices.
  • To run the live game, process actions, store progression, and synchronize multiplayer state.
  • To protect the game from abuse, fraud, cheating, service disruption, and account misuse.
  • To troubleshoot bugs, monitor performance, and improve balance, reliability, and usability.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important service notices.

4. Authentication and third-party services

Google Firebase Authentication provides guest and email/password accounts in the native apps. The browser game also offers Google, Twitch, Discord and email-link sign-in where available. The provider receives the information needed to authenticate you; we do not store your password on our game servers. Game hosting and storage use Hetzner Cloud in Germany.

The browser game uses Google Analytics for acquisition and sign-in events. Google Analytics is not initialized in the native apps. Both versions send first-party gameplay, session and diagnostic events to our game servers. Reporting a bug or suggestion may deliver the report and attached game diagnostics to our private Slack support workspace. Do not include passwords or sensitive information in a report.

5. How long we keep data

We retain your account and persistent game progress while you use the service. First-party session and acquisition records are pruned after 180 days and are also subject to row limits. Operational logs are rotated to limit their size.

Use Settings → Account → Delete account, or the account-deletion page, to submit a verified deletion request. Your current Firebase login is removed when the request is accepted. We complete removal or anonymisation of associated profile, account bindings, gameplay history, social data and account-linked telemetry within 30 days. Shared worlds and match outcomes may remain after account identifiers and personal names have been removed. Deletion cannot be undone.

Backup copies are isolated from normal use and expire within 30 days after completion. If a backup is restored, deletion requests must be reapplied before the service accepts players. A hashed deletion marker and receipt may be retained for up to 45 days to prevent token reuse and support safe restoration. Support correspondence and security records may be retained where necessary to resolve the request, prevent abuse or comply with law; contact us for details about a specific request.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the game, such as hosting, authentication, storage, analytics, and support tools. We may also disclose information if required by law or if reasonably necessary to protect our users, services, or legal rights.

7. In-game visibility

Certain information is visible to other players as part of normal gameplay, including your display name, territory, alliances, combat outcomes, leaderboard placement, and other game-facing state. This visibility is part of the core multiplayer experience.

8. Your choices

  • You may choose which sign-in method to use where available.
  • You may stop playing at any time, though some account data may still be retained as described above.
  • You can initiate permanent account and associated-data deletion directly in the app or on the deletion page, without a support call or email.
  • You may request access, correction, portability or restriction of personal data, or object to processing where those rights apply, by emailing our support address. You may complain to the applicable data protection authority.

9. Children’s privacy

Border Empires is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may provide notice in the game, through the website, or by other reasonable means.

11. Contact

Contact Benjamin Waye at benjamin@borderempires.com. You can also visit Border Empires Support.
