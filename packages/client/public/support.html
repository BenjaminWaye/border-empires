Help with your empire

Contact

Email benjamin@borderempires.com for account help, privacy requests or a problem with the Android, iOS or browser game. Border Empires is operated by Benjamin Waye.

For a bug, include the platform, what happened and the time. Never send your password, sign-in tokens or payment information. You can also use Settings → Diagnostics & Support → Report Bug in the game.

Delete an account

Use Settings → Account → Delete account inside the game, or open the account-deletion page. You can submit the request directly; no support email is needed. Unsaved guests must use the device where they played.

Connection and saved progress

The game requires internet access. Check your connection and reopen the app after an outage. An unsaved guest can only be recovered from the same app installation; save your empire to an email/password account before uninstalling or clearing app data.
