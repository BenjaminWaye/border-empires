5. How long a sector runs

  • A hosted sector runs one Sector Contest. There is no fixed end date: it runs until a House wins.
  • After the contest ends, the sector stays open for 3 days so its members can look at the final map. Then its server is deleted.
  • If no human player visits a sector for 7 days, it is closed and its server is deleted, with no look-around period. We email the sector's members 2 days before this happens. AI Houses playing on do not count as a visit.
  • When a sector is closed, the live world is deleted. The final results are kept and shown on members' profiles, marked "Hosted". A sector closed before its contest ended shows each member's standing at closure, marked as not finished.
  • We may restart a sector briefly to update the game.
