1. Who is responsible for your data

Border Empires is operated by TIDN AB, company registration number [[ORG_NUMBER]], [[REGISTERED_ADDRESS]], Sweden. VAT number [[VAT_NUMBER]]. Email: benjamin@borderempires.com. TIDN AB is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share information when you create an account, sign in, play the game and make a purchase.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information such as your email address, authentication provider, display name, and Firebase account identifier.
  • Gameplay information such as territory ownership, progression, resources, alliances, messages, combat outcomes, and other in-game state tied to your account.
  • Technical information such as browser type, device type, IP address, coarse location inferred from network data, and diagnostic logs necessary to operate, secure, and improve the game.
  • Support information you choose to send if you contact us with bug reports or account issues.
  • Purchase information if you buy something: what you bought, when, the amount, the currency, your country, and a payment reference from our payment provider. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Hosted sector information: which hosted sectors you host or have joined, and the invite links you create.

3. How we use your information, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the game and your account, and to deliver what you buy: this is needed to perform our contract with you.
  • To protect the game from abuse, fraud, cheating, service disruption and account misuse, to troubleshoot bugs, and to improve balance, reliability and usability: this is our legitimate interest in running a fair and working game.
  • To send you emails about your game (for example an attack on your territory, or your sector being ready). You can turn these off in the game or with the unsubscribe link in each email. Emails needed for a purchase, such as a receipt or a notice that your sector will close, are part of our contract with you.
  • To keep accounting records of purchases and to handle tax: this is a legal obligation.

4. Service providers

We use these companies to run Border Empires. They process personal data on our behalf or as independent controllers for their own services:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited) for sign-in and for usage analytics. When you sign in with Google, Twitch, Discord or email, that provider and Firebase process your login information under their own policies.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) for the servers the game runs on, including the server of a hosted sector.
  • Stripe Payments Europe, Limited (Ireland) for payments. Stripe collects your payment details directly and uses them to process the payment, prevent fraud and meet its own legal duties, under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend for sending email.
  • Slack for operational alerts to our team, which can include a player or sector identifier.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and gameplay data: for as long as you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise it as described on the account deletion page.
  • Purchase records: 7 years after the end of the financial year of the purchase, as Swedish accounting law requires. This applies even if you delete your account.
  • The live world of a hosted sector is deleted when the sector is closed. A backup copy of that world is kept for up to 30 days. The final results are kept.
  • Diagnostic logs: for a limited time needed to find and fix problems.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with the service providers in section 4 so they can do their part in running the game. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or if it is reasonably necessary to protect our users, our services or our legal rights.

7. In-game visibility

Certain information is visible to other players as part of normal gameplay, including your display name, territory, alliances, combat outcomes, leaderboard placement, and other game-facing state. This visibility is part of the core multiplayer experience.

Hosted sectors. A hosted sector is private in the sense that only its members can enter and play. It is not listed publicly. Its members see each other as in any Sector Contest, and the host can see how many places are taken.

Public House profile. You can choose to share a public web page for your House by pressing Share profile on your own profile in the game. Nothing is published until you do. Anyone with the link can open the page without signing in. It shows your display name and your game results: the Sector Contests you finished, your placements, rating, House Charges held, records and awards. It does not show your email address, your sign-in details or your account identifier, and the link does not contain them. If you delete your account, the page stops working when your deletion request is accepted.

8. Your rights and choices

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, where we have no legal duty to keep it;
  • object to processing that we base on our legitimate interests, or ask us to restrict it;
  • receive the data you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw a consent you have given.

To use these rights, email benjamin@borderempires.com. You can also delete your account on the account deletion page, choose which sign-in method to use, and decide whether to share a public House profile.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), imy.se, or to the data protection authority of the country where you live.

9. Children’s privacy

Border Empires is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may provide notice in the game, through the website, or by other reasonable means.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email benjamin@borderempires.com.
