## Summary

The drafts are sound in structure. They follow the EU, Swedish and UK rules for
a service started at once, and the code already does most of what they promise.
Five things must change before sales open. Two are in the texts and were fixed
here. Three are for the code or the owner.

1. **Cosmetics text was missing.** Written here as Terms of Sale §7a.
2. **UK GDPR representative.** A controller with no UK establishment that offers
   a service to people in the UK must name a UK representative (UK GDPR
   Art. 27). Continuous sales are not "occasional", so the exception is
   unlikely to apply. The privacy draft now has a `[[UK_REPRESENTATIVE]]`
   marker, which blocks publishing until it is filled in. Owner action.
3. **Emails.** The confirmation email is not built (plan 8c). For cosmetics it
   is a legal condition: without the emailed confirmation the buyer keeps the
   right to withdraw and owes nothing (Directive 2011/83/EU Art. 16(m)(iii)
   and 14(4)(b)(iii)). For withdrawals, the acknowledgement must be on a
   durable medium (Art. 11a). A cosmetic must therefore not be granted before
   its confirmation email has been sent.
4. **Analytics consent is not built.** Analytics run with no consent today. The
   privacy draft promises consent. Plan 8f must land before the page is
   published.
5. **Results are not saved, and hosted-sector member data is not deleted.** The
   terms and the privacy draft promise both. Plan 8d, plus the account-deletion
   script for the hosting database.

None of the above is a reason not to sell. Each has a clear fix.

It covers every point in the README's "Cosmetics" list:

| Point | Where in §7a | Basis |
|---|---|---|

| Consent and withdrawal for digital content supplied at once | "Your right to withdraw": request, acknowledgement and emailed confirmation; if any is missing, a full refund | Art. 16(m) and 14(4)(b); lag (2005:59) 2 kap. 11 §; UK CCR 2013 reg. 37 `[web search — verify]` |
| A refund removes the item | "Refunds" | — |
| Suspension and deletion | "If your account is closed" | Terms of Service |
| No advantage in play | first bullet, and §2 | `gdd.md` §8 hard rule |

Two wording choices to note. First, the right to change how an item is drawn is
limited to "recognisably the same item and not made worse". A broader right
would likely be unfair under Art. 19 of Directive 2019/770. Second, "Hosted
sectors do not show cosmetics yet" is there because each server has its own
cosmetics database (`cosmetics.md`, "Ownership and selection"). Promising
"shown everywhere" would be untrue today.

**Owner question (new, Q13):** should a cosmetic bought shortly before a
closure announcement be refunded, like a hosted sector less than 30 days old?
Recommendation: yes, for anything bought in the 30 days before the
announcement. It costs little, and it removes the strongest argument that a
closure term is unfair. Until the owner answers, §7a promises only the notice.
