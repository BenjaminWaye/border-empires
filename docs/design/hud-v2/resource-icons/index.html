Resource Icon Trials

The six resource icons in five versions: today's emoji, a hand-drawn SVG set built in the style of the existing structure overlays, and three AI image models given the same prompt. Each cell shows 48, 24 and 16 px. 16 px is the size used inline in tile menus and upkeep lines.

Chosen set

Gold, Food, Titanium and Umbrite come from Nano Banana 2. Crystal is Nano Banana Pro's aether orb in its brass gimbal. Shard is Nano Banana Pro's crystal cluster, recoloured white so it doesn't clash with the cyan crystal. These are the final cutouts (transparent PNGs, 256 px masters plus 96, 72 and 48 px exports), shown on both backgrounds.

What each approach costs

Hand SVG

  • About 1–2 KB per icon; sharp at any size, and colours can be changed in code.
  • Matches the flat brass look of the 122 existing overlay SVGs.
  • Less texture and depth than the AI art.

AI raster

  • Richer material: worn brass, patina, glass glow.
  • Needs a cleanup pass per icon (cutout, glow trim, 2× and 3× PNG exports), and fine detail turns to mush at 16 px.
  • Keeping the style consistent across ~90 icons means generating them in themed sheets, not one by one.

A hybrid option

  • Hand SVG for the 16–24 px glyphs (inline text, tabs, map badges).
  • AI art for the large emblems (structure info, tech cards), where its detail shows.

Raw AI sheets

Same prompt for all three: 6 resources in one row, futuristic steampunk, brass, copper, verdigris and aether glow. 24 Meshy credits, plus 6 for the shard redraw.
